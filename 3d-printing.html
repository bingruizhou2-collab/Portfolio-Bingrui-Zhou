PROJECT 02 / SUPPLY CHAIN

3D Printing & Supply Chain Design

Researching how additive manufacturing affects product design, sourcing, and production choices.

TYPE

Team course presentation

FOCUS

Case research · supply chain impact · presentation

The challenge

Our group examined the impact of 3D printing on supply chain design. My contribution focused on finding concrete applications and explaining their operational implications in a presentation.

My approach

  • Compared examples in aerospace and medical manufacturing, including additively manufactured aircraft components and specialized surgical tools.
  • Connected part consolidation and design flexibility to potential changes in supplier coordination and assembly.
  • Explained how smaller production runs can affect inventory and lead time decisions, while noting that the economics depend on the part and process.

What I learned

A technology's value depends on the full process around it. Evaluating manufacturing choices requires attention to sourcing, quality, cost, and delivery together.

This summary describes a class research project; it does not claim independent verification of manufacturers' performance figures.

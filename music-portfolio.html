PROJECT 03 / WEB & STORYTELLING

Music Portfolio Website

A multimedia site that connects my musical journey, band experience, and creative influences.

TYPE

Individual course project

FOCUS

Site structure · multimedia · reflection

The challenge

I wanted visitors to understand more than a list of performances. The website needed to show how I developed as a guitarist and how collaboration shaped my music.

My approach

  • Organized the site into Home, About Me, My Music Journey, Band Experience, Music Influences, Media, and Reflection pages.
  • Used performance and rehearsal media to support the written story.
  • Revised the layout and media descriptions in response to instructor feedback.

What I learned

Media works best when it has a clear purpose. Visitors need context, navigation, and short explanations that connect each clip to the larger story.

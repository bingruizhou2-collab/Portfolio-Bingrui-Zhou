PROJECT 01 / DATABASE DESIGN

Parking Lot Database Design

A relational design for parking lots, employees, their vehicles, and access permissions.

TYPE

Individual course assignment

FOCUS

Requirements · ER diagram · relational design

The challenge

A company with gated lots needed a way to track each lot's capacity and description, employees' contact information and assigned lots, and the vehicles employees may park. The design had to represent the rules clearly before implementation.

My approach

  • Identified the main entities: parking lots, employees, and vehicles.
  • Modeled each employee's regular permission for exactly one lot and each employee's ownership of one or more vehicles.
  • Organized keys, attributes, and relationships in an ER diagram and database design outline.

What I learned

Good database design starts with business rules. Making cardinality and required relationships explicit helps prevent inconsistent records later.

This is a project summary. The assignment document and diagram are not posted publicly because they contain course work.
